Final-Year Research Project

The Impact of Social Engineering on Cybersecurity

A research study examining how social engineering tactics compromise digital security, grounded in the Knowledge-Attitude-Behaviour model. Analyzed 74 survey responses across multiple universities, finding that trained respondents fell for simulated attacks at less than a third the rate of untrained respondents, and that voice/SMS scams outranked email phishing as the dominant local threat vector.
